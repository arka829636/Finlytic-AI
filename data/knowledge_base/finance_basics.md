\# Personal Finance Knowledge Base



\## Emergency Fund



An emergency fund is money kept aside for unexpected and necessary expenses, such as medical costs, urgent repairs, temporary loss of income, or other financial emergencies.



The purpose of an emergency fund is to reduce the need to borrow money when an unexpected expense occurs.



A common budgeting guideline is to build several months of essential living expenses as an emergency reserve. The appropriate amount depends on a person's income stability, essential expenses, dependents, insurance coverage, and other circumstances.



\## Budgeting



A budget is a plan for how income will be allocated across expenses, savings, and financial goals.



A simple budgeting workflow is:



1\. Calculate reliable income.

2\. List essential and discretionary expenses.

3\. Compare planned spending with actual spending.

4\. Set savings goals.

5\. Review the budget periodically.



A budget should be treated as a planning tool rather than a guarantee that future spending will match the plan.



\## Savings Rate



Savings rate can be calculated as:



Savings Rate = (Income - Expenses) / Income × 100



For example, if income is ₹50,000 and expenses are ₹40,000, savings are ₹10,000 and the savings rate is 20%.



The savings rate is only meaningful when the income and expense period being compared are consistent.



\## Needs and Wants



Needs are expenses required for basic living or essential obligations. Examples can include basic housing, food, utilities, transportation, and required healthcare.



Wants are discretionary expenses that may improve comfort or enjoyment but are not essential for basic needs.



The distinction is personal and can vary by situation.



\## Debt Management



When managing debt, it is useful to understand the interest rate, outstanding balance, minimum payment, fees, and repayment schedule.



Paying only the minimum payment can result in a longer repayment period and greater total interest for many types of debt.



A person should review the terms of their specific debt before making major repayment decisions.



\## Financial Safety



Personal finance information is educational and general. Financial decisions can depend on individual circumstances, tax rules, laws, contracts, risk tolerance, and other factors.



FinanceAI should not invent facts that are not present in the user's transaction data or knowledge base. When important information is missing, it should say so.
